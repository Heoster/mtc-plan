Registration roadmap

How Maples Academy gets registered

Eight phases, in order. Google, Microsoft and GitHub all verify a school through its domain name - and the school already owns one, so the part that normally costs money and takes weeks is done. Each phase is small, and the school may halt after any phase without loss.

Read this first

Almost every free education programme in the world verifies a school the same way: it looks for an institutional email address on a domain the school owns. Maples Academy already owns mapleskhatauli.com. Nothing has to be bought and nothing has to be renewed. What remains is to prove to Google that the domain belongs to a recognised school, and then to set up the Admin Console. After that the rest is form-filling.

Activate the domain we already own

The school owns mapleskhatauli.com. Google does not care what the suffix is. It cares that the school controls the domain and that the school is a recognised educational institution. Both are true today.

0

The Principal names two teachers

A Faculty Advisor to supervise the club's sessions, and a Coordinator to hold the Google Admin Console and act as the school's verified contact. These may be the same person. A line in writing from the Principal is enough.

1

Find out who controls the domain

Somebody bought mapleskhatauli.com and somebody renews it. The school office, or whoever built the present website, will have that login. This is the one thing to confirm before anything else starts.

2

Check whether email already runs on it

If the school already receives mail at an address ending in @mapleskhatauli.com, say so during the Google sign-up so the existing mail is not interrupted. If there is no email on the domain yet, Google simply becomes the school's mail provider. Either way it works - the office just has to tell the Coordinator which it is.

3

Add Google's verification record

Google supplies a short TXT record. It is pasted into the domain's DNS settings through the same login found in step 1. The existing website keeps working exactly as before; a verification record changes nothing a visitor can see.

What the school office must confirm first

This proposal cannot tell you two things from outside, and both should be checked before the Coordinator begins. One: who holds the account that mapleskhatauli.com was registered through, and whether the renewal is paid up. Two: whether any email is already running on that domain, because the sign-up asks. Neither answer blocks the plan. They only decide which path the Coordinator takes on the sign-up form, and five minutes with the school's records should settle both.

Documents to keep ready

Google, Microsoft and GitHub all ask for roughly the same proof that we are a real school. Scan these once, as PDFs under 2 MB each, and the same folder serves every application.

From the Principal

  • A line in writing from the Principal naming the Faculty Advisor and the Coordinator.
  • A letter on school letterhead confirming that the Coordinator is authorised to act for the school online. Google and Microsoft occasionally ask for this.
  • The login for the domain account, or somebody from the office available to add the verification record on the Coordinator's instruction.
  • Teacher identity proof - an identity card or an employment letter. GitHub and Canva both ask a teacher to prove employment.

Proof of the school's standing

  • Affiliation / approval / recognition letter from CBSE, ICSE, a State Board or other recognised authority.
  • Registration certificate establishing the legal constitution of the school or of its parent society or trust.
  • Address proof issued by a Government authority, and the school's telephone number and student and staff strength.
  • GST certificate, only if the school has one. None of the free programmes here require it.

No stamp paper, no posted originals and no fee are involved in any of this. Those belong only to the optional .edu.in route described further down.

Google Workspace for Education, and the Admin Console

  1. Open the Education Fundamentals sign-up and start an application as the school's administrator. Supply the school's name, type, website, student and staff numbers, address, telephone and a contact email that is not on the new domain.
  2. Enter mapleskhatauli.com and prove ownership by adding the verification record Google provides to the domain's DNS settings.
  3. Upload the accreditation document - the CBSE or Board affiliation certificate - when Google asks for evidence of educational status.
  4. Wait for review. Google assesses eligibility; applications are typically decided within a couple of weeks.
  5. Set up the Google Admin Console. This is phase 3 and it is the part that matters most. From the Console the Coordinator issues addresses in the form name@mapleskhatauli.com, groups users by class, sets the data privacy and content-filtering rules, and decides which Google apps each class may use. The Console stays the school's permanent control panel; the Principal and the Coordinator hold its passwords.
  6. Open Google Classroom for each class, and the club will run a short session for teachers who want one.

Microsoft 365 Education (Office 365 A1)

  1. Begin the education sign-up on the Microsoft Education site using an address on the school domain.
  2. Complete academic verification. If the domain is not automatically recognised, Microsoft asks for proof of the school's academic status - supply the affiliation certificate. Approval can take a few business days.
  3. Assign the free A1 licences from the Microsoft 365 admin centre: Users → Active users → Assign licences. There is no cap on the number of staff and student licences.
  4. Set up Teams for classes and staff, and enable OneDrive for each user.

Two practical cautions. The free A1 tier gives web and mobile Office apps - installable desktop Word and Excel require the paid A3 tier. And if Microsoft asks for a card during sign-up as an anti-abuse check, the A1 tier itself still remains free; read each screen carefully before confirming anything.

GitHub Education

Two separate applications: one by the teacher, one by each student.

The Coordinator applies as a teacher

  1. Create a personal GitHub account.
  2. Go to Settings → Billing & plans → Education benefits and start an application as a Teacher.
  3. Select the school, allow the location prompt, and upload a photograph of the faculty identity card or an employment verification letter, using the school email address.
  4. On approval, open GitHub Classroom and claim the Teacher Toolbox.

Each student applies for the Student Pack

  1. Be 13 or older and hold a personal GitHub account (organisation accounts do not qualify).
  2. Apply at education.github.com/pack and choose I am a student.
  3. Verify with the school email address or upload a dated school identity card, class schedule or enrolment letter.
  4. Decisions usually arrive within a few days. Verification is re-checked periodically, and individual partner offers renew on their own schedules.

Tip that prevents most rejections: when asked for the institution, match the exact spelling used in GitHub's school list, and make sure the identity card photograph clearly shows the student's name, the school's name and a date.

Would a .edu.in address be better?

This is the one question worth answering properly, because an earlier draft of this proposal was built around it.

ERNET India, an autonomous society under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, is the exclusive registrar for .edu.in, .ac.in, .res.in and .school.in. A CBSE-affiliated school is directly eligible, and an address ending in .edu.in carries visible academic standing in India.

But the school does not need it. Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Canva, Figma, JetBrains and Notion all verify a school by checking that it controls its domain and is a recognised institution. None of them require a particular suffix. mapleskhatauli.com satisfies every one of them, today, at no cost.

So the honest recommendation is this. Activate what the school already owns first. Get the accounts running, let a year pass, and see whether anyone misses the .edu.in. If the school then decides it wants that standing, the route is below and the earlier work is not wasted - a second domain can be added to the same Google Workspace without rebuilding anything.

1

Check availability

Search the name on the ERNET registry. It must match the school's full name or a recognisable abbreviation. Generic names and personal names are refused, and it must not resemble any Government body's name.

2

Prepare the papers

An application letter on school letterhead and an undertaking on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, both in ERNET's own format, signed and stamped by the Head of the Institution, plus the appointment letter of the administrative contact, the affiliation letter, the registration certificate of the school or its society, and a Government address proof. All as PDFs under 2 MB.

3

Apply, pay and post

Complete the online form and pay by card, net banking, NEFT or UPI. Demand drafts are no longer accepted. True copies of the stamped undertaking and the application letter go by post to ERNET India, New Delhi.

4

Await manual verification

ERNET reviews the documents by hand. Allow two to four weeks. Renewal needs fresh documents, so keep copies of everything.

Fees change - confirm before paying

Published ERNET tariffs have varied over the years and differ between sources. The figure quoted here (≈ ₹1,180 for one year, inclusive of GST) is indicative only. Please confirm the current tariff on the ERNET portal at the time of applying, and note that registering several years at once reduces the yearly cost. A restoration fee applies if a domain is allowed to lapse. None of this affects the main plan, which costs nothing.
