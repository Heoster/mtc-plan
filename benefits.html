Artificial intelligence

AI tools — what is actually available to us in India

This is the section most often exaggerated online, so it is written carefully.

Available to us, free, today

  • OpenAI Academy — free AI-literacy courses worldwide, including a K–12 educator track.
  • Free tiers of ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot for supervised classroom demonstration, with a teacher present.
  • Microsoft Learn AI paths and Kaggle Learn — free, structured, certificate-bearing.
  • CBSE's own AI skill-subject material, which the club will use to support classroom teaching.

Not available to us — stated plainly

  • OpenAI's free ChatGPT for Teachers plan is United States only. It is genuinely free for verified U.S. K–12 educators, but it is not offered to Indian schools at present.
  • Most headline “free AI for students” offers require the user to be 18 or above and are aimed at college students, not school students. Google's student offers change periodically — always read the current eligibility.
  • ChatGPT Edu is an institutional, paid licence aimed at universities and large districts, not at individual schools.

How the club will actually use AI

The club teaches students to understand AI: what these systems are, where they get things wrong, how to check them, and why handing in their output as your own work is dishonest. All of it is supervised, on approved free plans. Under the club's rules, submitting AI work without saying so is treated as copying. That rule is written into the code of conduct every member signs.

Government of India

Schemes the school itself can apply for

Atal Tinkering Laboratory — up to ₹20 lakh

Under the Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog, a selected school receives grant-in-aid of ₹10 lakh to establish the laboratory (3D printer, robotics and electronics kits, sensors, microcontrollers, tools) and a further ₹10 lakh towards operating expenses over five years.

Open to schools with Classes VI–XII that can spare the built-up space AIM asks for. Applications open in announced windows — watch the portal.

aim.gov.in/atl.php →

ERNET India — optional academic domain

ERNET India, an autonomous society under the Ministry of Electronics and IT, is the exclusive registrar for .edu.in, .ac.in, .res.in and .school.in. A CBSE-affiliated school is directly eligible.

We do not need this. The school already owns mapleskhatauli.com, which every programme here accepts. A .edu.in address is a credibility upgrade to consider later, at roughly ₹1,180 a year.
